# Privacy

The app does not declare internet access and does not upload camera frames, audio, looks, or telemetry. Camera and microphone permissions are used for capture. Bluetooth access is optional and limited to the selected bonded peer for foreground control; no video or audio is transmitted by the remote protocol. On older Android versions storage permission is needed to save media. Gyroscope data is optional and stored in a clip-related local CSV. Look files, take metadata and recording journals are stored locally. Exporting/sharing files sends them only through Android's user-selected destination. Device reports contain manufacturer/model and camera capabilities, not a unique device serial or location. Uninstalling may remove app-private look and journal data; export what you need first. Shared media is managed by Android MediaStore.
